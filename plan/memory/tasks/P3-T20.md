## Deviations
Spec prescribed two separate `useEffect` hooks (one for chatId reset, one for processing). Combined into a single `useEffect` with a chatIdRef-based reset check at the top to satisfy Biome's `useExhaustiveDependencies` lint rule — the reset-only effect would have `chatId` in deps but not in the body, which Biome flags. Single-effect approach is simpler, equally correct, and lint-clean. Body is ~25 lines instead of ~20 due to the stream-cleared guard (matching oldapp's DataStreamHandler behavior), which is within the "~20" approximation.
