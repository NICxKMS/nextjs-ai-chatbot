## Section Reasoning
- Core chat and auth workflows are mostly representable under slim-route, guard composition, repository, and streaming patterns.
- Most partial/uncovered items cluster around policy precision (who owns capability rules), artifact naming consistency, and frontend runtime constraints not captured by architecture docs.

## Section Conclusion
For B001-B032, architecture fit is strong for foundational flows, but high-risk gaps remain around capability policy centralization and artifact/document contract finalization.
